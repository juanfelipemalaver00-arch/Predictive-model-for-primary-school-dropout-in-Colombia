Education Data Integration: Crosswalk & Join Architecture

A technical overview of how the final school-year panel is constructed, which databases are crossed, the keys used at each stage, and how information flows into the final analytical dataset.

1. Executive overview

The pipeline builds a longitudinal school-site × year master panel. The backbone is created from C-600 “Jornada/Nivel” information and uses SEDE_CODIGO × PERIODO_ANIO as the primary key. The remaining C-600 modules are attached horizontally using the same school-site/year key.

Municipality-level educational outcomes from SIMAT are first standardized and aggregated at the municipality-year level. DIVIPOLA acts as the geographic crosswalk that converts standardized municipality and department names into the official five-digit DANE municipality code. Those SIMAT indicators are then merged into the school panel through COD_MPIO_DANE × PERIODO_ANIO.

IPM is integrated at the region × year level, while PDET and ZOMAC are municipality-level enrichment flags. ICFES is integrated using a two-step strategy: a direct school-site × year match first, followed by an establishment-level rescue for observations that remain unmatched.

4. Source-specific integration logic

C-600: construction of the school-year backbone

The pipeline searches the C-600 files for the Jornada/Nivel skeleton for each year from 2018 to 2023. SEDE_CODIGO is standardized to a 12-digit string, and enrollment is converted to numeric form. If a total enrollment field is unavailable, the code constructs total enrollment by summing available male and female enrollment fields.

The raw records are aggregated to SEDE_CODIGO × PERIODO_ANIO × COD_MPIO_DANE × COD_DPTO_DANE. The resulting SEDE × YEAR key is explicitly checked for duplicates before the master panel is created.

Remaining C-600 modules

Each additional C-600 source is read separately for all panel years, standardized on SEDE_CODIGO, converted to numeric counts, aggregated to site-year, and left-merged into the backbone. Male and female counts are retained, and a total is calculated for each module.

SIMAT + DIVIPOLA: municipality-level outcomes

SIMAT is restricted to municipality territory, official-sector observations, and the panel years. Municipality and department names are normalized by upper-casing, removing accents, trimming whitespace, and collapsing repeated spaces.

Dropout and repetition records are aggregated to municipality-year. Rates are calculated as weighted averages using the SIMAT TOTAL as the weight. DIVIPOLA is then used as the official geographic crosswalk: standardized municipality + department names are matched to obtain the five-digit COD_MPIO_DANE. These municipality-year indicators are finally attached to every school site in the corresponding municipality and year.

IPM: regional context

IPM household files are loaded for each panel year. The numeric region identifier is mapped to a named DANE region. Available deprivation variables are aggregated by region × year using FEX_C as the household weight. The school panel receives the same regional measures through a department-to-region mapping and the key REGION_DANE × PERIODO_ANIO.

PDET and ZOMAC: municipality enrichment

Both sources explicitly use COD DANE. Codes are converted to five-digit strings, invalid codes are removed, duplicates are collapsed, and a binary municipality flag is created. These flags are then merged into the school panel using COD_MPIO_DANE.

ICFES: direct match plus establishment-level rescue

ICFES DANE identifiers are normalized to exactly 12 digits. The panel's school code is also normalized, including the special handling required when the panel code appears with an extra leading digit.

Step 1: ICFES is matched directly using school-site code × year. Step 2: observations that remain without a global ICFES score are rescued using the establishment-level table. Establishment-level aggregation sums student counts and averages the other available measures across sites.

7. Validation and quality controls

  • Primary-key validation: duplicate SEDE_CODIGO × PERIODO_ANIO observations trigger an error.
  • Period validation: observations outside 2018–2023 trigger an error.
  • Geographic validation: candidate municipality codes are compared with DIVIPOLA, PDET and ZOMAC code sets.
  • Coverage diagnostics: coverage is reported for SIMAT, IPM, ICFES and selected variables, including coverage by year and by TRAIN/TEST sample.
  • Missingness report: the final diagnostic table reports null counts, null percentages, means, minima and maxima for variables.
  • Target diagnostics: Pearson correlations with municipality dropout and lag-1 autocorrelation are calculated.
  • 2023 test diagnostics: the code separately reports 2023 row count, unique sites, target coverage and lag-1 coverage.
Train/test design: 2018–2022 are classified as TRAIN, while 2023 is classified as TEST. Temporal features are constructed within each school site after sorting by site and year.

8. Final analytical structure

The resulting dataset is organized into logical blocks: keys and geography, target variables, temporal lags, enrollment dynamics, C-600 composition and vulnerability, ICFES variables, IPM regional context, and PDET/ZOMAC enrichment.

The final pipeline exports the master panel in both Parquet and CSV formats and produces an Excel diagnostic workbook containing completeness, correlations, coverage by year, train/test coverage, descriptive statistics, unmatched SIMAT observations and a 2023 sample.
